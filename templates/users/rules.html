🎯 Game Rules

Predict the exact result of each match. Points are awarded based on how close your prediction is to the actual result. Only one category applies per prediction (the highest one you qualify for).

Points Category Example
6 pts Exact result Predicted 2:1, Result 2:1 ✓
5 pts Correct tendency + goal difference Predicted 3:2, Result 1:0 (both +1 diff)
4 pts Correct tendency + one team's goals Predicted 3:2, Result 3:0 (home goals match)
3 pts Correct tendency only Predicted 3:2, Result 7:0 (both home wins)
1 pt One team's goals only (wrong tendency) Predicted 3:2, Result 0:2 (away goals match)
0 pts No match Predicted 3:2, Result 0:0

Note: For knockout matches, the result includes extra time (if played). Penalty shootouts don't count, so you can predict a draw in knockout rounds.

Your base points are multiplied depending on the tournament phase. The later the round, the more valuable each prediction!

Round Multiplier Max Points (exact)
Group Stage ×1 6 × 1 = 6 pts
Round of 32 ×2 6 × 2 = 12 pts
Round of 16 ×2 6 × 2 = 12 pts
Quarter-finals ×3 6 × 3 = 18 pts
Semi-finals ×3 6 × 3 = 18 pts
Third-place match ×3 6 × 3 = 18 pts
Final ×3 6 × 3 = 18 pts

The 2026 World Cup has an expanded format with 72 group stage matches. To make predictions more strategic, you can only predict 36 of these matches.

⚠️ Choose wisely!

You can only predict half of the group stage matches (36 out of 72). Pick the matches where you feel most confident. Any predictions beyond this limit won't count.

Tip: There's no limit on knockout stage predictions—you can predict all of them!

Jokers let you double your points on selected predictions. Use them strategically on matches where you're confident!

Final Points = Base Points × Round Multiplier × Joker (×2)

Example: Exact result in Quarter-finals with Joker = 6 × 3 × 2 = 36 points!

Joker Distribution

Round Jokers Available
Group Stage None (no jokers)
Round of 32 3 jokers
Round of 16 3 jokers
Quarter-finals 2 jokers
Semi-finals, 3rd place & Final 2 jokers (combined)

Important: Once a match starts, you can no longer add or remove the joker. Set your jokers before kickoff!

Before the tournament starts, predict which team will win the World Cup. If you're right, you get bonus points based on your team's odds category!

Category Teams Bonus Points
Category A Top 8 favorites (by betting odds) 20 points
Category B All other teams (underdogs) 30 points

🏆 Risk vs Reward

Picking a favorite (Category A) is safer but gives fewer bonus points. Picking an underdog (Category B) is riskier but rewards you with 30 points if they win!

Deadline: You must select your champion before the first match of the tournament!

Your total score is the sum of all your match prediction points (with multipliers and jokers) plus any champion bonus.

Tiebreaker Rules

If two or more players have the same total score, we use these criteria in order:

  1. Number of exact predictions — More exact results (6 base points) ranks higher
  2. Number of jokers used — More jokers used ranks higher (shows more commitment)
  3. Shared rank — If still tied, players share the same rank
Example Ranking

1. Alice — 100 pts, 8 exact, 5 jokers

2. Bob — 95 pts, 7 exact, 5 jokers

2. Charlie — 95 pts, 7 exact, 5 jokers

4. Diana — 90 pts, 6 exact, 4 jokers

Bob and Charlie share 2nd place (same points, exact predictions, and jokers).

📖 How to Use

How to Enter Predictions

  1. Click "Tipps" in the navigation bar
  2. Find the match you want to predict
  3. Enter your predicted score for both teams
  4. Your prediction is saved automatically

⏰ Deadline Warning

You can only enter or edit predictions before the match starts. Once kickoff begins, your prediction is locked!

Remember: In the group stage, you can only predict 36 out of 72 matches. Choose wisely!

How to Use Jokers

  1. Go to "Tipps" and find a knockout match
  2. Look for the joker icon (🃏) next to your prediction
  3. Click the joker icon to activate it for that match
  4. The icon will highlight when active

💡 Tips for Jokers

  • Jokers are only available in knockout rounds (no group stage)
  • Each round has a limited number of jokers
  • You can see how many jokers you have left for each round
  • Remove a joker by clicking the icon again (before kickoff)

Important: Jokers lock when the match starts—just like predictions!

How to Pick Your Champion

  1. Click your username in the navigation bar to go to Settings
  2. Find the "Predicted Champion" section
  3. Select a team from the dropdown list
  4. Click Save to confirm your choice

⚠️ Critical Deadline!

You must select your World Cup champion before the first match of the tournament starts. After that, you cannot change it!

Your champion pick will be shown next to your name on the leaderboard with the team's flag.

Understanding the Leaderboard

The home page shows the current rankings. Here's what each column means:

  • # — Your current rank (ties share the same rank)
  • Name — Username with their predicted champion's flag
  • Points — Total score including multipliers and jokers
  • Exact — Number of exact predictions (6 base points)
  • Jokers — Number of jokers used so far

💡 Tip: You can filter the leaderboard by tournament round using the dropdown menu. This lets you see how everyone was doing at any point in the tournament!

Viewing Other Players' Predictions

On the Tipps page, you can see what other players predicted for each match.

  1. Go to "Tipps" in the navigation
  2. Click on any match card
  3. A panel shows all predictions for that match

🔒 Privacy Protection

You can only see other players' predictions after the match has started. This prevents copying predictions before kickoff!
